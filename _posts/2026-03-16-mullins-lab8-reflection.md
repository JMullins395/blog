Lab 8 was an interesting introduction to a possible practical application of the database concepts we have learned about up to this point.  These are my reflections.

### What were the easiest parts of the tutorial?

The easiest parts were definitely the more general commands, especially in the first part of the tutorial when I was building the "RazorPagesMovie" app.  The wording was pretty clear overall, and in the first phase of the lab I essentially only had to follow along, copy, and paste.  It was very straightforward for the most part.

### What were the confusing parts of the tutorial?

The confusing part came when I had to create my own website as a kind of customized version of the tutorial.  There was one specific issue where a dependency required for one of the later steps was half-buried in the notes for that section of the tutorial, leading to a fair bit of dismay.

### What was difficult to translate when creating your own application?

The most difficult bits of the translation when transitioning to the second phase of the lab were having to thoroughly check over all of the commands and "copy this" code to use my new terminology (i.e. "Automobile" instead of "Movie"), as well as the slightly differing arrangement I used for the database elements (leading to some extrapolation beyond just following along with the tutorial).

### What parallels and differences do you see between the Jekyll and Razor frameworks?

The biggest parallel I can see between Jekyll and RazorPages is that they provide a generalized, customizeable template for their given purposes.  Their executions, purposes, and capabilities seem more varied though.  Jekyll provides more of a blank slate to build up from, while RazorPages (as executed in this lab at least) provides you with much more of a guiding scaffold to build from.  Jekyll, as I have used it at least, seems more tailored towards blogging and the like, while RazorPages feels more geared towards creating sites that are designed to allow the user to interface with a backend database, be it for reading or editing.  They both provide an easier way to build applications than simply starting from complete scratch, but they are different horses for different courses.

### How confident do you fell making another Razor application?

I think I would feel pretty confident making another RazorPages application with similar functionality to the tutorial app and my custom derivative, as of now.  Going beyond that scope, however, feels quite a bit more shaky at the moment, and I will need some more practice and experience I think.

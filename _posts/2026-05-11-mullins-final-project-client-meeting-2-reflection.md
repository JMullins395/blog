Following the second client meeting, our plan for the project underwent a significant reformation.  From the start, we had some considerable issues getting the project off the ground.  After a few unsuccessful turns of the crank, we arrived at the conclusion of using a Razor Pages application as the base.  From here though, there were still considerable issues with our original 3-view idea.  It was at this point that we shifted from making the student information entry apparatus as a built in part of the website to instead opting for a PDF embed system.  This allowed us to progress without omitting important information.  With the views effectively reduced to two, we were able to proceed through to the end of the project in this configuration.  Additionally, this was the stage at which we first developed the "bare minimum" schema that stripped away everything except core functionalities necessary to meet Ms. Simmons' stated needs.  This would be our guiding blueprint until the late stages of the project.

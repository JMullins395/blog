### Talents and Skills that were useful during this project

In the computer science-focused context of this particular project, I believe that some of the most valuable skills to have had for this project were, of course, related to having an aptitude for the applied aspect of the coding itself.  However, something that I believe was equally as valuable of a tool to have on hand for this project was the ability to bridge the gap between the needs of our client - Ms. Simmons - and our abilities to build something to help make her important role less strenuous to carry out.  Throughout the project, I consistently, and frustratingly, found myself taking more time than I would have liked to get a solid enough grip on some aspects of the project to be able to proceed in a way that would not impede my fellow group members, and yet I was nonetheless still able to eventually do so.  Moreover, I believe that I was able to make the best use of the abilities that I had, and some new ones that I honed over the course of the project, to advance as far as possible towards fully realizing the goal of making something that would be useful to Ms. Simmons, and applicable to her work.

### Impact on Social Change

From the very first client meeting, it was clear that the main goal for this project, the main service we were to render, was to help make Ms. Simmons' workflow much less tedious and time-consuming.  The conversation in that meeting made it very clear that much of the Med School application process, despite the strides made since the days of physical folders making their rounds about campus from faculty member to faculty member over the course of months, that a huge amount of the work and tedium still fell directly on the shoulders of Ms. Simmons and the Med School application committee.  Although the application that we have built is still a bit shy of *fully* functional, it is a great start that can probably be finished with (relative) ease by a subsequent development team - and when that happens in the not-too-distant-future, Ms. Simmons' life in this regard will have been made *much* easier.  The work Ms. Simmons and the committee are doing is a great help to the pre-med students applying to med school, propelling them along the path to be able to help many people.  By undertaking this project, and making strides towards making Ms. Simmons' job easier, I think we have thus by extension helped those who benefit from the work that she does, and those people (the pre-med students) will then go on to help even more people.  It is a beautiful social example of the "butterly effect" in its infancy, and I am happy to have played a part.

### Effects on Values and Ideals

This project has allowed me to much more clearly and palpably see the potential of the skills I have been learning not merely as technological instruments with which to enable to convenience of modern society, but as potent tools with which to help someone or make someone else's life easier than it would have been otherwise.  I was able to glean quite a bit of joy from the payoff at the end of this project, seeing how pleased Ms. Simmons was with our work, how grateful she was for our efforts in improving an aspect of her work that, while important, had been more than a little bit of a drudge up to this point.  I used nearly every piece of the wide array of topics that was covered in this class' coursework, and to make something that will tangibly help someone, no less.  This project greatly increased my conviction that the skills I have learned thus far, and those that I have yet to learn, will not simply be left on a shelf after I graduate, but will instead become useful tools for good in my future career.

### Reshaping of Personal Views

I must admit that, before this project, my main goal with this major was undoubtedly to simply be able to get a job to make a living in a field that I did not despise, so as to set myself up for a pleasant life after college.  However, having used my burgeoning computer science accumen to *really* help someone, I believe that I will seek out more opportunities to do the same in the future.  After all, if this knowledge can make my life easier and more pleasant, then why can my use of them not be used to do the same for others?  Having been in a position during this project where my unique set of skills was able to significantly aid someone whose requests for assistance might have otherwise gone unfulfilled has given me a new newfound sense of responsibility and duty to be the first one to step forward when my set of skills can be used to help someone in any situation that I might encounter in the future.
